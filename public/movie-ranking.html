The Best Movies According to PB

The top 3 movies ever (based on my Letterboxd)

The Long Goodbye

Philip Marlowe was the inspo for Spike Spiegel but idgaf about that. I personally find this movie relatable as someone who has been roped into unsavory situations but managed to get out of them with just a scratch or two. (not illegal)


Man on the Moon

Andy Kaufman has inspired absurdist comedy and internet memes. If you like absurdist comedy and internet memes, watch and learn from the godfather of it all!


Ghost World

Growing up is a pain. Changes are a pain. I missed out on being more of an asshole, choosing to take the high road instead of being more upset, and more scummy. Enid is the asshole I wanted to be as a kid. I love the graphic novel this movie was based on.
